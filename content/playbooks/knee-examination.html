Knee Examination Playbook

A full knee screen in the order you would perform it, with every value marked for what it is worth. The knee's recurring lesson is that a test's accuracy depends on more than the test: the ligament figures come from patients pre-selected for surgery, McMurray's accuracy moves with time since injury and differs sharply between orthopaedic and primary care hands, and the lever sign's result shifts with the surface the leg rests on. Two graded guidelines cover meniscal and cartilage lesions and arthroplasty; ligament management has none, and section 10 says so rather than borrowing confidence it does not have. This is a study aid for examination preparation, not a clinical reference - check any value against current literature before it weights a decision about a patient.

How to read a value. Unmarked means traced to a source you can read for free, with the citation beside it. Convention nobody has tested it. Contested tested, and the studies disagree - quote the range.

01

The competency checklist

check-off saves in this browser

Thirty-seven capabilities, grouped by the question each phase of the examination answers. Two of them come before any special test: is this a knee problem at all, and does it need a radiograph before it needs a hand on it. Every item is written as something you can perform rather than something you can name, and the last column is the finding - the number or observation that turns the manoeuvre into information, with what it is worth beside it.

#ItemHow it is performedFinding / norm
Should I be examining this knee at all?
1 Screen the hip and lumbar spine Lumbar quadrant and segmental mobility, then hip flexion-adduction-internal rotation and passive hip IR. Ask whether the knee symptom changes. Do this first. Knee pain refers from both, and a knee examined for referred pain reads normal and reassuring. Convention The whole-limb version does carry a grade: "a focused musculoskeletal exam of the lower extremities should be performed when assessing for an ACL injury" - Strong, on high-quality evidence AAOS 2022.
2 Apply the Ottawa Knee Rule Five criteria; any one positive sends the knee for a radiograph before it gets a special test. A rule-out, and only that. Pooled Sn 0.98 (0.96-0.99), −LR 0.12 (0.05-0.26) across 18 studies and 6,702 patients Kazemi 2023. The five criteria are not reproduced on this page: the meta-analysis these figures come from reports the rule's accuracy without restating its content, so take the criteria from the rule itself rather than from here.
3 Red flag screen Fever, night pain, calf swelling, prior cancer, and whether they can actively extend the knee against gravity. An acutely hot, exquisitely painful knee with fever is not an examination problem. A patient who cannot actively extend the knee against gravity but can be moved there passively has an extensor mechanism to exclude before anything else is worth doing. Convention
4 Classify from the history Traumatic or atraumatic, mechanical or inflammatory - and how long ago. The last of those is not a formality at the knee. McMurray moves from Sn 91 / Sp 55 at 5-7 days to Sn 80 / Sp 79 with a PPV of 92% at 4-5 weeks Rinonapoli 2025. Time since injury changes what a test is worth, not merely how confident you feel.
What is the resting state?
5 Standing alignment Weight-bearing, from front and side: varus, valgus, recurvatum. Compare limbs. Convention No threshold that makes an alignment abnormal is traceable here. no traceable source Record it as a description you can repeat, not as a diagnosis you have made.
6 Effusion, graded Modified stroke test for small effusions, ballottement for large. Grade it and compare sides. Use the modified stroke test specifically: it is named as an essential physical impairment measure to document at baseline and at follow-up Logerstedt 2018. Its reliability is not reported anywhere reachable - searched twice. no traceable source
7 Quadriceps bulk and vastus medialis Girth at a fixed distance above the patella, and VMO definition, both sides. Atrophy is a chronicity marker, not a diagnosis. Convention The measure that counts is strength, not appearance - maximum voluntary isometric or isokinetic quadriceps testing is the essential one Logerstedt 2018.
What can they do on their own?
8 Gait, including stance-phase control From the front and the side: quadriceps avoidance, varus thrust, and whether the knee reaches extension at heel strike. Convention No free accuracy figures for gait observation at the knee. no traceable source Use it to generate the hypothesis you then test, never to confirm one.
9 Active flexion and extension Goniometry, both knees, same landmarks, recorded as numbers. Knee active ROM is an essential measure to document Logerstedt 2018. Its norms are not: measured against a national probability sample, normative hip and knee values differed from textbook estimates by as much as 18°, while the gap between the youngest and oldest age groups was only 3 to 5° Roach 1991. The other knee beats any table.
10 Extension lag versus fixed flexion contracture Take extension passively, then ask for it actively, and compare the two. Passive exceeding active is an extension lag - a quadriceps problem. Both equally limited is a contracture - a joint problem. Convention Two different treatments follow, which is why this one distinction does more work than the rest of the section combined.
11 Squat and step-down Depth, symmetry, frontal-plane control at the knee, and whether the symptom reproduces. Its value is that it repeats within a session, which makes it a reassessment marker rather than a diagnosis. Convention Watch the hip while you watch the knee - a valgus collapse on step-down is often a hip finding wearing a knee's clothes.
12 Functional composites and hop testing 30-second chair stand, stair climb, timed up-and-go and 6-minute walk early; single-leg hop tests later. That is the named set Logerstedt 2018. Hop testing "may be considered as one factor to determine return to sport" - Strength of Option Limited AAOS 2022. No symmetry cutoff is established; see item 23.
What can I do to them?
13 Passive range against active Take each direction passively and compare with what they produced actively. Passive exceeding active points to inhibition or weakness; both equally limited points to the joint. Convention The size of that gap is the finding, not the passive number on its own.
14 End feel, including a springy block Take each motion to its limit and name what stops it: capsular, bony, springy, empty, guarding. A springy block to extension is the classic displaced-meniscus finding, and it changes the urgency of everything after it. Convention Taught universally; no reachable reliability at the knee. no traceable source
15 Patellar mobility and tilt Glide medially and laterally in slight flexion; assess tilt against the femoral condyles. Convention No traceable threshold for what counts as hyper- or hypomobile. no traceable source Compare sides and state what you felt rather than grading it against a number that does not exist.
16 Accessory motion at both joints Tibiofemoral anterior and posterior glide at 20-30°; patellofemoral glide in slight flexion. Grade resistance, compare sides. Convention Graded resistance is universally taught and has no reachable reliability figure at the knee. no traceable source It is a comparison, not a measurement.
What is short?
17 Quadriceps length Prone knee bend, or Ely's test. Watch the pelvis, not the heel. The compensatory pelvic rise is the finding students miss: it means you have run out of quadriceps and the rest of the range is coming from the lumbar spine. Convention Stop measuring at the rise, not at the heel.
18 Hamstring length Passive straight-leg raise, or 90/90 knee extension with the opposite thigh flat on the table. Convention No threshold or reliability figure reachable at the knee. no traceable source Hold the opposite thigh down, or you are measuring pelvic tilt.
19 Gastrocnemius, because it crosses the knee Ankle dorsiflexion with the knee straight, then with it bent. The difference between the two is the gastrocnemius contribution - bending the knee takes it off stretch and leaves soleus. Convention A knee examination that never touches the ankle misses it entirely.
What is weak?
20 Quadriceps - and whether it is weak or inhibited Dynamometry for a number that repeats. Then reduce pain and effusion and retest. A grade that improves at once was inhibition, not weakness, and the rest of the plan depends on which it was. The one graded intervention aimed squarely at inhibition is daily NMES to the quadriceps at the highest tolerable intensity after arthroplasty, at moderate evidence quality Bove 2026 - an intervention for inhibition, not for load.
21 Hamstrings, and the hamstring-to-quadriceps relationship Prone knee flexion against resistance, or dynamometry at matched speeds. Record the relationship rather than either number alone. Convention No reachable threshold for a normal ratio. no traceable source
22 Hip abductors, every time Side-lying or standing with the pelvis controlled, so the trunk cannot supply the force. The knee follows the hip in the frontal plane. The graded recommendation is for progressive strength training of the knee and hip muscles together - B Logerstedt 2018, not the knee alone.
23 Limb symmetry, and what it does not license Compare involved to uninvolved as a percentage, on strength and on hop distance. ContestedHop testing may be considered as one factor for return to sport - Limited AAOS 2022. No cutoff is established, and the guideline names the disagreement rather than hiding it: the studies behind it tested at 6 months, at a mean of 8.1 months, at 9 months and at 12 months, and disagree on whether waiting longer lowers graft failure. One input among several, never the gate.
Where is it?
24 Joint line, tibiofemoral Knee flexed to about 90°; palpate the line medially and laterally, separately. Sensitive and almost non-specific: Sn 0.77 (0.68-0.84), Sp 0.26 (0.18-0.36), OR 1.16 (0.63-2.13) - an interval that includes 1. Reliability poor, κ ≤ 0.38. And it reads differently by sex: in men Sn 0.50-0.58 with Sp 0.74-1.00; in women Sn 0.40-0.49 Logerstedt 2018.
25 Patellar borders, tendon, tibial tubercle Borders and facets with the knee relaxed, then the tendon and the tubercle. Convention Localises rather than grades. Tenderness at the tubercle in a skeletally immature athlete is a different problem from tenderness at the tendon in an adult, and palpation is what separates them.
26 Collateral origins and insertions Walk the medial and lateral collaterals from origin to insertion, and palpate the medial joint line separately from the MCL. Separating MCL tenderness from medial joint line tenderness is the whole point - they sit millimetres apart and implicate different structures. Convention Tenderness narrows the differential and confirms nothing on its own.
What is it?
27 Ligament tests Lachman at 20-30°, anterior drawer at 90°, pivot shift, lever sign; varus and valgus stress at 0° and 30°. Lachman Sn 87.6 / Sp 88.9; anterior drawer 77.5 / 86.1; pivot shift 65.9 / 94.4 Nguyen 2025. Every one of those comes from 165 patients already indicated for arthroscopy, so expect worse in a clinic. Varus and valgus stress have no reachable accuracy at all. no traceable source
28 Meniscal tests, read against time since injury McMurray, Apley, Thessaly and joint line tenderness - after you have asked when the injury was. McMurray with Apley gave the best accuracy and minimised false positives of six tests in 255 patients Rinonapoli 2025. Three of the four primary-care odds ratios include 1 - Thessaly, Apley and joint line tenderness. Only McMurray's excludes it, and only just, at 1.04 to 3.09 Logerstedt 2018.
29 Patellofemoral tests Use them to reproduce and localise the symptom. Not to confirm a diagnosis. no traceable source No reachable accuracy exists for any bedside patellofemoral test - searched twice, and the absence is the finding.
30 Interpret as likelihood ratios, and cluster from the hypothesis Read each result as a shift in probability, and choose tests that fail in different ways. The familiar bands are a teaching heuristic, not a traced value. Convention What is sourced: the Ottawa rule's +LR of 1.56 with an HSROC AUC of 0.54 makes it a rule-out whatever its authors' summary says Kazemi 2023. Two tests sharing a mechanism give you one test twice.
What do I call it?
31 State a movement diagnosis Name what the knee does under load that reproduces the symptom, and in which direction. A structural label tells you what is irritated; a movement diagnosis tells you what to change. Convention
32 Recognise the syndromes, and their limits ACL rupture, meniscal tear, patellofemoral pain, osteoarthritis, tendinopathy. Two of them have no reachable bedside criteria at all - patellofemoral pain and knee OA. no traceable source Naming those two stays clinical judgement; managing OA is graded in detail AAOS 2021. Knowing which half of a diagnosis is evidenced is part of making it.
33 Identify relative stiffness above and below Look at the hip and the ankle before you treat the knee. A knee that will not flex borrows from both. Convention Motion goes where it is easiest, which is why the painful segment is often the mobile one compensating for a stiff neighbour - and why treating the painful site alone tends to fail.
What do I do about it?
34 Dose from irritability Grade reactivity - pain at rest, night pain, and how long a flare takes to settle - before choosing intensity. Convention at the knee. no traceable source The published irritability classification is a shoulder instrument. Borrowing its logic is reasonable; borrowing its validation is not, and the guide will not pretend otherwise.
35 Match intervention to impairment For each intervention chosen, name the impairment it addresses. If you cannot name the impairment an intervention targets, it is in the plan for the wrong reason. Convention
36 Apply the graded guideline that fits Identify which of the four covers what you are treating, then read the grade and the harm statement together. Meniscal and cartilage Logerstedt 2018, osteoarthritis AAOS 2021, ACL AAOS 2022, arthroplasty Bove 2026. For OA, exercise is Strong and manual therapy added to it is Limited - two grades apart, which is the ranking to hold on to when a session runs short.
37 Define the reassessment marker Before treating, name the one measure you will recheck and what result would change your mind. Prefer one of the essential measures - modified stroke test, active ROM, quadriceps strength, joint line tenderness Logerstedt 2018 - because those are the ones you already have a baseline for. A marker chosen after the fact is a justification; one chosen in advance is a test.

Almost every number here is an accuracy figure rather than an anatomical constant, which is why each one is worth less on its own than the four notes underneath the grid.

Read every figure above with its population attached. The three ligament values come from 165 patients already indicated for arthroscopy Nguyen 2025; the meniscal ones from 292 patients and 75 healthy controls seen in primary care Logerstedt 2018. High pretest probability and a narrow spectrum inflate accuracy. Neither set is wrong; quoting either without its population is.

The examiner moves these numbers further than the choice of test does. McMurray reads Sn 91 · Sp 55 in orthopaedic hands in a trauma cohort with roughly 74% prevalence, and Sn 58 · Sp 56 in primary care in a mixed population - a wider gap than sits between any two tests in this section. Section 07 turns that into the questions to ask before running anything.

Check the interval before the point estimate. Three of the four meniscal odds ratios include 1 - Thessaly, Apley and joint line tenderness. Only McMurray's excludes it, and only just, at 1.04 to 3.09 Logerstedt 2018. And the lever sign's result depends on the surface the leg rests on and where the examiner puts their fist, both significantly associated with the outcome, while effusion grade, force and pain were not Norris 2025.

Four values are missing from this grid because the knee has none. The Q angle has population-specific figures and no universal norm; knee osteoarthritis has no reachable bedside criteria set; no bedside patellofemoral test has published accuracy; and the hand effusion tests have no reliability figure. Convention no traceable source Each was searched twice. Limb symmetry for return to sport has no established cutoff either, and the guideline says so itself AAOS 2022.

Strength

At the knee the important question is usually not how weak the quadriceps is, but whether it is weak at all as opposed to inhibited. Answer that before you write a grade, because the two need opposite treatments and only one of them responds to load.

MusclePosition & what you stabiliseWhat weakness looks likeWhat fakes a result
Quadriceps Seated, knee near extension. Dynamometry where you have it - maximum voluntary isometric or isokinetic is the measure the guideline names Logerstedt 2018. Give-way against resistance, or an extension lag that appears only in the last few degrees. An effusion, and pain. Both suppress the quadriceps directly, so a grade taken over a swollen knee is a measurement of the swelling. Reduce them and retest before you believe the number.

Special tests

Two things make this section different from the shoulder's. The ligament figures come from a population pre-selected for surgery, and the meniscal figures move depending on when you examine. Both change how the numbers should be used, not merely how confidently.

Anterior cruciate ligament
Lachman The anterior cruciate, near the angle where it is the primary restraint to anterior translation and the hamstrings guard least. Supine, knee 20-30°, femur stabilised. Translate the tibia anteriorly; grade excursion and endpoint. Increased translation with a soft or absent endpoint against the other knee. The workhorse. Sn 87.6 · Sp 88.9 Nguyen 2025 - read with the population row below.
Anterior drawer The same ligament, at an angle where the hamstrings can guard and a meniscus can block the translation. Knee at 90°, foot fixed, tibia drawn forward. Increased anterior translation compared with the other side. Sn 77.5 · Sp 86.1 - less sensitive than Lachman for the same structure, which is the argument for preferring Lachman Nguyen 2025.
Pivot shift The rotational instability an ACL-deficient knee actually complains of, rather than translation alone. Valgus with internal rotation, taken from extension into flexion; feel for the reduction clunk. A palpable clunk as the tibia reduces - not pain. Rules in, cannot rule out. Sn 65.9 · Sp 94.4 Nguyen 2025. Hard to perform on a guarding patient, which is part of why the sensitivity is low.
Lever sign The anterior cruciate, using the tibia as a lever over your fist instead of a manual translation. Supine, fist under the proximal calf, downward pressure on the distal thigh. Watch the heel. The heel fails to lift off the table. ContestedOne study gives 93.8 / 94.4 Nguyen 2025; another, in a general traumatic-knee population, found κ 0.529 (0.368-0.691), at best +LR 3.02 and −LR 0.45, and called it "limited clinical utility" Norris 2025. Its result also moves with the surface the leg rests on and where you place your fist - both significantly associated with the outcome, while effusion, force and pain were not Norris 2025.
Meniscus
McMurray The meniscus, compressed between the condyles as the knee extends under rotation. Flexion with rotation, then extension, palpating the joint line throughout. Pain or a palpable click at the joint line. Time-dependent: Sn 91 / Sp 55 at 5-7 days, Sn 80 / Sp 79 with PPV 92% at 4-5 weeks Rinonapoli 2025. In primary-care hands, Sn 0.58 / Sp 0.56 Logerstedt 2018. The examiner and the timing move it further than the manoeuvre does.
Apley The same compression, with the tibia loaded axially and the femur held by the patient's own weight. Prone, knee at 90°. Compression with rotation, then distraction with rotation. Pain on compression that eases on distraction. Alone, barely better than a coin: Sn 0.53 · Sp 0.53, OR 1.24 (0.73-2.12) - an interval including 1 Logerstedt 2018. Paired with McMurray it is a different proposition: that combination gave the best accuracy of six tests and minimised false positives Rinonapoli 2025.
Thessaly The meniscus under weight-bearing rotation - the loading the patient's own symptoms come from. Standing on the affected leg, knee flexed to about 20°, rotating internally and externally. Joint line discomfort, or locking and catching. Discriminates poorly: Sn 0.66 · Sp 0.39, OR 1.24 (0.71-2.18) Logerstedt 2018. Its reliability is the best of this group at κ 0.54, fair - against κ ≤ 0.38 for McMurray and joint line tenderness. Repeatable and uninformative are not the same thing.
Joint line tenderness Not a manoeuvre - direct palpation of the meniscal rim. Knee flexed to about 90°; palpate the medial and lateral lines separately, and separately from the collateral. Tenderness localised to the line rather than to the ligament beside it. Sensitive, almost non-specific: Sn 0.77 · Sp 0.26, OR 1.16 (0.63-2.13). Reliability poor, κ ≤ 0.38. It also reads differently by sex - men Sn 0.50-0.58 with Sp 0.74-1.00, women Sn 0.40-0.49 Logerstedt 2018.
Collateral ligaments and patellofemoral joint
Varus and valgus stress The collaterals at 30°; the collaterals plus cruciates and capsule at 0°. That is why both angles are tested. At 0° and again at 30°, comparing opening and end feel with the other knee. Increased opening, or a soft end feel where the other side is firm. No reachable accuracy for either angle. no traceable source Graded by comparison rather than by a cited figure - and opening at 0° implicates more than the collateral, which is the whole reason for the second angle.
Patellofemoral tests The patellofemoral joint, loaded or compressed to reproduce the patient's own pain. Varies by test; all of them aim to reproduce and localise rather than to stress a discrete structure. Reproduction of the presenting symptom, in the place the patient points to. No. no traceable source No reachable accuracy exists for any bedside patellofemoral test - searched twice. Use them to reproduce and localise; the absence of a number is itself the finding.

Syndromes and what you call it

The knee's syndromes are easier to name than to confirm. The last column is the one worth reading twice: for two of the five, nothing in the examination confirms the label, and for one of those the management is graded in detail even though the diagnosis is not.

SyndromeWhat you seeWhat confirms itWhat the evidence actually supports
ACL rupture A pivoting mechanism, immediate effusion within hours, and instability on the history rather than only pain. Nothing in the examination alone. Lachman screens; imaging or arthroscopy confirms. Screened with Lachman Nguyen 2025; confirmed by neither the examination nor the lever sign Norris 2025. The graded recommendation is for the examination to happen at all - history plus a focused lower-extremity exam, Strong on high-quality evidence AAOS 2022.
Meniscal tear Mechanical symptoms - catching, locking - with joint line pain and an effusion that came on over a day rather than an hour. McMurray with Apley, read against time since injury. That pairing gave the best accuracy of six tests and minimised false positives Rinonapoli 2025. Management is graded: progressive ROM, knee and hip strengthening and neuromuscular training, all B Logerstedt 2018.
Patellofemoral pain Anterior knee pain with loading, stairs and prolonged sitting; often no effusion and no mechanical symptom. Nothing. It is a diagnosis of pattern and exclusion. No bedside test has reachable accuracy. no traceable source Searched twice. Name it from the pattern, and say out loud that the label is clinical rather than tested.
Knee osteoarthritis Age, load-related pain, morning stiffness that eases, crepitus, and a gradual loss of extension. No reachable bedside criteria set. no traceable source Unlike the hip, there is no Grade A rule to apply. Checked rather than assumed: the OA guideline's stated purpose is "to evaluate the current best evidence associated with treatment", so it grades what to do and says nothing about how to recognise it. Naming it is clinical judgement; managing it is graded in detail - exercise, self-management and patient education all Strong AAOS 2021. See section 10.
After arthroplasty A post-surgical timeline, precautions, and a quadriceps that is inhibited rather than weak. The operation note, not the examination. The best-evidenced management in this guide: progressive strength training taught to the patient from the early postacute period, and daily NMES to the quadriceps at the highest tolerable intensity Bove 2026.

Palpation

Palpation comes late deliberately. Palpating first tells you where a patient is tender; palpating after the movement examination tells you whether that tenderness explains anything. The last column is the honest one - at the knee, the only palpation finding with published accuracy is also one of the least specific things you can find.

LandmarkHow to find itWhat tenderness there implicatesWhat that tenderness is worth
Tibiofemoral joint line Knee flexed to about 90° so the line opens. Walk it medially and laterally, and stay off the collateral - they sit millimetres apart. The meniscal rim, medial or lateral. Sensitive, almost non-specific: Sn 0.77 (0.68-0.84), Sp 0.26 (0.18-0.36), OR 1.16 (0.63-2.13) - an interval including 1. Reliability poor, κ ≤ 0.38. And it differs by sex: men Sn 0.50-0.58 with Sp 0.74-1.00, women Sn 0.40-0.49 Logerstedt 2018.
Patella, tendon, tibial tubercle Borders and facets with the knee relaxed and the quadriceps off; then the tendon from pole to insertion, then the tubercle. Patellofemoral structures, patellar tendon, or the apophysis in a skeletally immature athlete. Convention Localises rather than grades. Tubercle tenderness in a growing athlete is a different problem from tendon tenderness in an adult, and palpation is what separates them.
Collateral origins and insertions Walk each collateral from origin to insertion, then palpate the medial joint line separately from the MCL. The collateral ligament itself, as against the meniscus beneath it. Convention Narrows the differential and confirms nothing on its own. Separating MCL tenderness from medial joint line tenderness is the entire point of doing it slowly.

The ceiling. Palpation cannot distinguish a tear from degeneration, and it cannot confirm any of the syndromes in section 08. Convention Use it to localise and to corroborate what a test already suggested - and note that the one figure available for it, joint line tenderness, has a specificity of 0.26 and poor reliability. Isolated tenderness with nothing else agreeing is a finding you should be willing to discard.

Mobilisation, exercise and dose

Four graded guidelines reach this section, not one. The knee's problem is not a shortage of graded management evidence - it is that the grades cover treatment while the examination that gets you there is mostly convention. Read the grade and the harm statement together: a grade says how much evidence exists, not how much the intervention is worth.

Rapid drill

Two ways to rehearse. The cases work the whole chain from presentation to reassessment marker; the single facts test one value at a time, and most of them test whether you know what a value is worth as well as what it is.

What to practice

Built from the cells you marked missed while running recall. Nothing here is pre-filled - if this is empty, go and quiz yourself first. The practice method is chosen by what the item is, not by what kind of learner you are: a number, a pair of hands and a decision are three different jobs and they need three different kinds of rehearsal.

Why this is not a learning-styles quiz. Sorting students into visual, auditory or kinesthetic learners and matching teaching to the label is one of the most durable ideas in education that the evidence does not support - the studies that test it properly find no benefit from the match Pashler 2008. What does hold up is the method fitting the material: retrieving a fact from memory beats re-reading it, and spacing the attempts beats massing them Dunlosky 2013. So the plan below sorts your misses by the kind of thing they are.

Cases - work the whole chain

Open a case, then work down the prompts - answer each out loud and click to check. Answers are hidden every time a case opens, so nothing is spoiled by looking.

Footballer, 22. Twisting injury four days ago, swelled within an hour, felt a pop. Lachman feels soft with a vague endpoint. McMurray reproduces medial joint line pain.
Ottawa first
Apply it before anything else. Sensitivity 0.98 with a −LR of 0.12 means a negative rule genuinely lowers fracture probability; a positive one barely raises it, since +LR is 1.56 and the AUC is 0.54 Kazemi 2023.
What Lachman buys you
Sn 87.6%, Sp 88.9% - the balanced ligament test Nguyen 2025. Discount it slightly: those figures come from patients already indicated for arthroscopy.
The McMurray trap
At four days you are in the T1 window, where McMurray is Sn 91% but Sp only 55% Rinonapoli 2025. A positive here is weak evidence. The same positive at four to five weeks, with specificity up to 79% and PPV 92%, would mean considerably more.
Would the lever sign settle it?
No. Contested One study gives Sn 93.8%/Sp 94.4% Nguyen 2025, another only moderate agreement and +LR 3.02 in a general population Norris 2025 - and its result shifts with the surface and your fist position.
What to do
Do not chase a diagnosis from tests this early. Settle irritability, protect, restore extension, and re-examine at three to four weeks when the meniscal tests are worth more.
Teacher, 61. Six weeks after an uncomplicated total knee replacement. Active extension lacks 12°; passive extension is full. Quadriceps looks wasted. She has been given a brace to wear at night and a CPM machine to hire.
Lag or contracture?
Passive exceeds active, so this is an extension lag - a quadriceps problem, not a joint one. Stretching the joint will not fix it.

Recommended against, at high evidence quality: no consistent long-term improvement in function, ROM or quality of life, and it may increase discomfort Bove 2026.

Single facts

The Ottawa Knee Rule has a sensitivity of 0.98. What is its specificity, and what does that make it good for?
Specificity 0.43. It is a rule-out and nothing else: +LR 1.56 barely moves probability, the HSROC AUC is 0.54, and the whole value sits in the −LR of 0.12 Kazemi 2023.
That paper's conclusion calls it "high diagnostic performance". Is it?
In one direction only. An AUC of 0.54 is close to chance discrimination. Take the phrase at face value and you will over-read a positive rule - read the ratios instead Kazemi 2023.
Give sensitivity and specificity for Lachman, anterior drawer and pivot shift. Which is the rule-in test?
Lachman 87.6/88.9, anterior drawer 77.5/86.1, pivot shift 65.9/94.4 Nguyen 2025. Pivot shift is the rule-in test - most specific, least sensitive.
Why should you discount all three slightly?
Because all 165 patients were already indicated for arthroscopy. High pretest probability and a narrow spectrum inflate accuracy; expect worse in clinic Nguyen 2025.
Two studies disagree about the lever sign. Give both results and the reason for the gap.
Contested Sn 93.8%/Sp 94.4%, "useful" Nguyen 2025, against moderate interrater agreement (κ 0.529) and at best +LR 3.02/−LR 0.45, "limited clinical utility" Norris 2025. The second says why: reviews favouring the test rest on studies whose limitations could overestimate its utility. One cohort was pre-selected for surgery, the other was not.
Two things change the lever sign's result that have nothing to do with the knee. What are they?
The surface the leg rests on and where the examiner puts their fist - both significantly associated with the outcome. Effusion grade, force applied and pain reported were not Norris 2025.
McMurray at one week versus one month - give both sensitivity and specificity.
5-7 days: Sn 91%, Sp 55%. 4-5 weeks: Sn 80%, Sp 79%, with PPV 92% Rinonapoli 2025.
Why does that matter for an acute knee?
Because a test's operating characteristics move with time since injury. A positive McMurray in week one is weak evidence; the same positive a month later is much stronger. Re-examining later is a diagnostic act, not a delay Rinonapoli 2025.
Which two meniscal tests combine best, and what does the combination buy you?
McMurray with Apley - best accuracy, minimising false positives, across 255 patients with 188 confirmed tears Rinonapoli 2025.
What is Thessaly's sensitivity?
Unknown here. no traceable source It was tested and compared but no accuracy figure appears in the free text; it was noted as more influenced by anterior knee pain Rinonapoli 2025. Any Thessaly figure you have seen quoted did not come from this study.
Extension lag or fixed flexion contracture - how do you tell, and what does each implicate?
Compare passive with active. Passive exceeds active = lag, a quadriceps problem. Both equally limited = contracture, a joint problem. Two different treatments follow, and it is the most useful distinction in the active section.
What is the normal knee range of motion?
There is no figure here you can defend. Convention no traceable source What is traceable: measured against a national probability sample, normative hip and knee values differed from textbook estimates by as much as 18° Roach 1991. Compare to the other knee instead.
What is a normal Q angle?
Not a defensible number. Convention Published values are population-specific, with no universal norm and no reliability study reachable no traceable source.
Which intervention after knee arthroplasty has high-quality evidence against it, and why?
Continuous passive motion. No consistent long-term improvement in function, ROM or quality of life, and it may increase patient discomfort Bove 2026.
What is the NMES recommendation after arthroplasty, and at what intensity?
Apply to the quadriceps at least daily, from the early postoperative period, at the highest tolerable intensity, for strength, gait and performance outcomes. Moderate evidence quality Bove 2026.
"Do not brace after knee replacement." Is that what the guideline says?
Not quite. It advises against routine early bracing to gain ROM, because it may prolong bedrest and restrict early mobility and gait training - but it also records that bracing into extension may have short-term effects on pain and function Bove 2026. Against routine use is not a prohibition.
Where does the limb symmetry threshold come from?
Nowhere reachable. Convention no traceable source Cohorts apply a cutoff; none found here establishes one. A threshold whose origin you cannot reach is a convention, not a measurement.
McMurray is reported at Sn 91% in one study and Sn 0.58 in another. Which is right?
Both, for their own setting. Rinonapoli used orthopaedic surgeons in a trauma cohort with roughly 74% prevalence Rinonapoli 2025; Blyth used primary care clinicians in a mixed population including healthy controls Logerstedt 2018. Same manoeuvre, different examiners, different people - and the gap between them is wider than the gap between any two tests.
Give Thessaly's sensitivity and specificity, and say what its odds ratio tells you.
Sn 0.66, Sp 0.39, OR 1.24 with a confidence interval of 0.71 to 2.18 - it includes 1, so the test barely discriminates. Its reliability is nonetheless the best of the three at κ 0.54, against κ ≤ 0.38 for McMurray and joint line tenderness Logerstedt 2018.
Joint line tenderness has a sensitivity of 0.77. Why is that less useful than it sounds?
Because its specificity is 0.26 and its odds ratio interval includes 1 Logerstedt 2018. It is sensitive and almost non-specific: a negative is somewhat informative, a positive is close to noise. It also performs differently by sex - in men Sp 0.74 to 1.00, in women Sn only 0.40 to 0.49.
Which knee problem has no graded guideline at all?
Ligament management. Meniscal and cartilage lesions have one Logerstedt 2018 and arthroplasty has one Bove 2026, but the knee ligament sprain guideline's free abstract is a purpose statement carrying no recommendation, grade, dose or threshold Logerstedt 2017.

Meniscal tests

  1. Rinonapoli G, Lucchetta L, Ancillai G, Manfreda F, Ceccarini P, Caraffa A Clinical reliability of 6 meniscal tests: a diagnostic accuracy study of 255 patients. Acta Orthop 2025;96:452-458255 patients aged 20-45, two blinded surgeons, tested at 5-7 days and again at 4-5 weeks, 188 arthroscopically confirmed tears. Source for McMurray's sensitivity falling from 91% to 80% while specificity rises from 55% to 79%, and for McMurray with Apley as the best combination. Carries no individual figures for Apley, Thessaly, Ege, joint line tenderness or hyper-flexion - those tests are compared but not quantified in the free text.

Range of motion

  1. Roach KE, Miles TP Normal hip and knee active range of motion: the relationship to age. Phys Ther 1991;71(9):656-665NHANES I national probability sample, 1,892 subjects. Covers hip and knee, so it is a knee source in its own right. Carries the finding that measured values differ from textbook estimates by as much as 18° and that age-group differences are only 3 to 5°. Does not carry per-motion normative figures; those tables are paywalled, which is why every ROM number here is marked convention.

About this guide. Version 1.0 · September 2026. The examination sequence, the section order and the wording are original. Every clinical value is drawn from the eight published sources listed above and attributed where it appears - principally Kazemi and colleagues 2023 for the Ottawa Knee Rule, Nguyen and colleagues 2025 with Norris and colleagues 2025 for the ligament tests, Rinonapoli and colleagues 2025 with Logerstedt and colleagues 2018 for the meniscal tests, Roach and Miles 1991 for range of motion, and Bove and colleagues 2026 for the whole of section 10. Values marked Convention are named as such because no research stands behind them, and five are flagged as having no traceable source at all: normative knee range, the Q angle, the accuracy of any patellofemoral test, the reliability of the clinical effusion tests, and the origin of the limb symmetry threshold. Two of those were searched twice before being flagged. Saying so is deliberate. Section 10 carries grades from two guidelines - meniscal and cartilage lesions, and arthroplasty. Ligament management has no freely readable graded source, and the section says so rather than implying otherwise. Verify against current literature before letting any of it weight a clinical decision.

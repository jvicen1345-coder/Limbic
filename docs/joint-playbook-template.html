Making it yours - your own lines, and importing from a PDF

The Taught lane is yours and starts empty. Nothing is pre-filled, because what your program teaches is yours to state rather than ours to guess. Double-click any box and a purple line opens beneath it - type what your instructor said. The guide's own text is never editable; your line sits under it, saves in this browser, and prints with the page.

Have your course handout as a PDF? Pull your program's wording straight into the taught lanes. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere - not to this site, not to anyone - and you review every suggestion before it lands.

Recall mode - how the quizzing works

Recall all in the nav blanks the page - every table column, callout, card and diagram label. The first column of each table stays readable, since it names what you are being asked about, as do headings and callouts. A section heading or a single table's own Recall hides just that answer column, and the Hide chips let you pick from there. On a diagram, click a label to lift that entry. Click a hidden cell to check it, mark the ones you miss, and Hide missed then blanks only those. Case answers in the drill re-hide every time a case is opened, so nothing is spoiled by looking.

The markers stay readable while an answer is hidden. A blurred cell still tells you whether what it hides is measured, convention or contested - so the question is not only "what is the number" but "how much is that number worth". A contested value has no single answer to recall; the range is the answer.

What to practice

Built from the cells you marked missed while running recall. Nothing here is pre-filled - if this is empty, go and quiz yourself first. The practice method is chosen by what the item is, not by what kind of learner you are: a number, a pair of hands and a decision are three different jobs and they need three different kinds of rehearsal.

Why this is not a learning-styles quiz. Sorting students into visual, auditory or kinesthetic learners and matching teaching to the label is one of the most durable ideas in education that the evidence does not support - the studies that test it properly find no benefit from the match Pashler 2008. What does hold up is the method fitting the material: retrieving a fact from memory beats re-reading it, and spacing the attempts beats massing them Dunlosky 2013. So the plan below sorts your misses by the kind of thing they are.

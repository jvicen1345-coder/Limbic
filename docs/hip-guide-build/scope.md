# Hip Examination Playbook — scope

The competencies the guide covers, in the order the examination is performed, grouped by the
question each phase answers. This is the author's decision, not the literature's: it is what a
student is examined on and the order a clinician actually works in. Sourcing applies to the
*values* inside each item, not to the choice of items.

Section shape matches the shoulder guide: checklist, numbers, posture, AROM, PROM & length,
MMT, special tests, syndromes, palpation, mobs & exercise, drill, refs.

## Should I be examining this hip at all?
1. Lumbar and sacroiliac screen
2. Red flag screen — fracture, SCFE in the adolescent, avascular necrosis, septic arthritis, malignancy
3. Classify the presentation from the history — intra-articular, extra-articular, or referred

## What is the resting state?
4. Standing posture and pelvic alignment
5. Leg length
6. Muscle bulk and atrophy

## What can they do on their own?
7. Gait
8. Single-leg stance and the Trendelenburg sign
9. Squat
10. Active range of motion, all planes
11. Functional composites — step-down, sit-to-stand

## What can I do to them?
12. Passive range compared to active
13. End feel
14. Capsular pattern
15. Accessory motion at the hip

## What is short?
16. Thomas test — iliopsoas, rectus femoris, tensor fascia lata
17. Ober test
18. Hamstring length
19. Piriformis length

## What is weak?
20. Abductor strength — gluteus medius and minimus
21. Extensor strength — gluteus maximus
22. Flexor and adductor strength
23. Grade on the 0-5 scale, and distinguish weakness from inhibition

## Where is it?
24. Bony landmark palpation
25. Soft tissue palpation — greater trochanter, adductor origin, ischial tuberosity

## What is it?
26. Perform the special tests by category — impingement, labral, intra-articular, extra-articular
27. Interpret likelihood ratios
28. Build a cluster from your hypothesis

## What do I call it?
29. State a movement diagnosis
30. Recognize the common syndromes
31. Identify relative stiffness

## What do I do about it?
32. Set the dose from irritability
33. Match intervention to impairment
34. Mobilize with a stated rationale
35. Define the reassessment marker
